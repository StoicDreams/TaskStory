## Questions

### What counts as a seat?

In Team plans, seats are charged based on active usage per month. Users are considered active if they use Task Story while signed in at least once during the billing cycle.

### What happens when I reach a free plan limit?

Everything you have stays. Task Story only stops you adding more of that kind of item until you remove some or change plans in My Subscription.

### What is story retention?

Stories older than your plan's story retention are deleted automatically.
