## Task manager

The [task manager](/tasks) documents your own work. Tasks are only visible to you, even when you are working in a team, so use the details as much or as little as you need.

A task needs at least one tag. Tag tasks with the project, problem, or solution they are for, so the tag filter shows them alongside that work.
