## Dashboard overview

Your [dashboard](/dashboard) is where you land when you sign in, and it is your hub for getting your documentation done quickly.

## Tag filters

The **Filter** bar at the top of the dashboard holds the tag filter. Its tags apply to every manager on the page, so each one shows only items that have all the filter's tags.

- Type a tag in the filter box and press Enter to add it to the filter. Remove a tag with its × button.
- Adding a tag with a prefix, such as `problem:homecontent`, replaces any filter tag with the same prefix, so you look at one problem or project at a time.
- The **Bug** and **Blocked** buttons add or remove the `bug` and `blocked` tags.
- The tag buttons in the bar turn a tag on or off. **More filters** opens the quick filters (Team, Project, Problem, Solution, Feature, and People), which choose the tags that get a button. Team and Project list all their tags when empty.
- Each manager has a **Filter by** button for the selected item, so you can jump from a problem to its solutions.

## Manager toggles

The manager toggles choose which managers appear on your dashboard: Tasks, Solutions, Problems, Features, Projects, and the Markdown guide. Each manager also has its own page in the menu.

## Managers

Each manager lists its items on the left and edits the selected item on the right.

- **Add** starts a new item. It starts with the tags in the current filter, so a solution added while filtering on a problem is linked to that problem.
- **Name** also sets the item's own tag, such as `solution:addcontent` for "Add Content".
- **Tag prefix** sets the prefix added to tags you type, such as `problem:`.
- **Editor**, **Both**, and **Rendered** switch between writing Markdown, writing with a preview, and reading.
- **Copy Markdown** and **Copy HTML** copy the whole item.
- With explicit saving, the title shows **(Changes Pending Save)** until you click **Save**, and **Undo** discards your changes. With automatic saving, changes save shortly after you stop typing. Choose on your [profile page](/account).
