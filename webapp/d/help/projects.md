## Project manager

The [project manager](/projects) documents the projects your team manages in Task Story. Each project gets its own tag, such as `project:taskstory`, which you add to features, problems, and solutions to group them under it.

Use the **Summary** tab for an overview, and the other tabs for detail:

| Tab | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Who will benefit from this project? |
| What | What is this project? What is the product it represents? |
| Where | Where is the product used? |
| When | When is the product used? When is it needed? |
| Why | Why is there a need for this project? Why will people use this product? |
| How | How will users discover the product? How will it be used? |

The project owner prioritizes the project's problems. See the [workflow](/help/workflow).
